⚔️ Outpost Duel

Two rival outposts, six rounds, one Influence track. Eight contested sites, each with a basic and an advanced tier — draft wisely, gamble on the Skirmish. Skill sets you up, the dice decide the margin.

How to play
Goal: Most Influence after 6 rounds wins.
Each round: draw 2 Tactic cards → draft the 8 board slots (3 picks each, snake order — 2 slots go unused every round, so choosing what to skip matters) → resolve a Skirmish if someone took the Garrison → resources cap and the round ends.
Board: every site offers a free Basic action and a pricier Advanced one with a bigger payoff — Market, Quarry, Garrison, Outpost, Archive, Foundry, Bazaar, Shrine. Eight sites, sixteen ways to play them.
Skirmish: the Aggressor may attack. Both sides commit troops (spent either way) and may play one hidden Tactic card as a modifier. 1d6 + troops + card modifier (+ Garrison Advanced bonus) per side — higher total wins Influence equal to the margin (capped at 4).
